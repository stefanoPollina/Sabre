CONTINUUM SABRE Sample chopper

Start with the original break, map its hits, then use the slice sequencer to build a new rhythm. The top display shows the actual source and playback state. Punch adjusts gate depth/open length and enables the gate above zero; Flow adjusts swing; Bite adjusts drive/cutoff bite and enables distortion above zero; Space adjusts echo send/feedback and enables echo above zero. These macros set existing parameters; they are not extra DSP stages. Turning Punch, Bite or Space to zero bypasses that section. Individual parameter changes remain available. Slice pads trigger processed one-shots; when the transport is stopped they bypass the rhythm gate. Starting or stopping the main transport clears pad previews. Add processing when the chops feel right.

Sample Deck and Slice Sequencer · editing and saved grooves

Load or drop a sample · click a region to select · drag its top handle to move a cut · double-click to add a cut · right-click a cut to remove.

Automatic roles are suggestions, not guaranteed drum recognition. Override them for your sample. Groove styles rearrange existing slices; they do not move your cut markers. Each step triggers one slice to keep choke-mode playback clear.

Saved grooves appear under Your Grooves. They store notes, bar length and swing—not audio. Matching slices must be available when recalled. Export a backup; browser storage can be cleared.

Waveform: drag a marker, double-click to add one, right-click an internal marker to remove it. Piano roll: click to place, drag to move, drag the right edge to resize. Slices map to notes from C2 upwards. Amber = selected for editing; violet = currently playing. Edit and randomise while playing. Changes reach upcoming hits within the 150 ms scheduling window; the transport keeps running. Fit-to-note changes pitch; one-shots can overlap when choke is off.

The Sample Deck and Slice Sequencer start closed. Open Sample Deck to access slicing, sample bars, BPM and tempo sync controls. The Slice Sequencer starts closed. Open its heading to access the piano roll and groove controls; closing the panel does not stop playback.

In the Sabre Sample Bank, open the amber folder names to browse subfolders, choose a sample, then press Load Bank Sample. The browser checks the repository when Sabre opens and uses its bundled catalogue if the check fails. New audio folders under SABRE BREAKS/Sabre_Breakbeats_Bank or SABRE BREAKS/Sabre_Processed_Beats are included on a successful refresh.

WUB WUB / tempo-synced bass movement

WUB WUB applies a resonant low-pass sweep and tape drive to your current sample, chopped slices or grains. It uses the existing final filter automation: the filter curve and live dial point show the sweep. Choose 1/4, 1/8 or 1/16 for one sweep per selected note value. BPM sets the timing. Sweep range, shape and resonance remain editable in Filter / Squelch. Click WUB WUB again to restore the filter, drive and modulation settings from before you applied it. Your audio, cuts, notes, gate and echo are preserved. Use bass-rich source material for a pronounced wobble; the preset does not generate a bass oscillator.

Granular synthesis and the three processing points

The Grain Field crosshair controls source position on X and position spray on Y. The smaller strip below shows grains actually playing. The amber scan marker follows the engine scan. Drag a dial vertically to adjust it; hold Shift for finer movement, use arrows for stepped changes, or double-click to reset. Filter automation moves the coloured dial point; the small hollow marker preserves the base setting. Response and echo plots show the existing filter and delay settings.

Select GRAINS, then Play. Grain size sets fragment duration; density sets fragments per second. Position sets the start of the scan, scan speed moves through the source independently of pitch, and spray scatters positions. Freeze holds the current scan point; changing Position relocates it. Short grains preserve rhythmic detail; longer overlapping grains build sustained textures.

Before granulation: enable Pre-effects, adjust drive, filter and echo, then Apply Pre-effects. The rendered source includes up to three seconds of echo tail. This changes the material read by grains without replacing your original sample. Pre-effects changes wait for Apply or the next playback start. A new sample invalidates the prepared material.

Per grain: pitch, pitch scatter, reverse probability, stereo spread and a resonant filter are applied separately to every fragment. Filter scatter varies cutoff in octaves. Grain level controls the cloud before the final rack; overlap compensation controls buildup.

After granulation: the original Sabre gate, filter, distortion and echo remain the final processing stage. Enable them independently. Dry audition always plays the original sample. CHOPS and pads use grains when “Granulate sequenced slices & pads” is enabled; slice clouds respect markers, note duration and velocity. Choke fades the preceding cloud.

All three stages are included in processed WAV export. Freeze and scan speed affect sample-length renders; fixed bar renders follow the selected tempo. Presets change grain controls only, leaving your existing arrangement and final effects intact. The display shows grain positions in the prepared material; the original waveform and slice markers stay unchanged.

01 · Quick start
  1. Choose Load Sample or drop an audio file onto the sample deck or waveform. To browse the sample library, expand Track Chopper, open Sample Repo at the bottom, choose Drums or Mixed Samples, connect, browse folders and select Load Into Sabre. Loading replaces the current source and its slice arrangement; files are fetched only on request. Library availability depends on bucket read policies, network access and project quota.
  2. Press Play. SABRE starts in Sample mode, looping the original audio with gate, filter, distortion and echo off.
  3. Choose a slice count, then Equal Slices for regular divisions or Detect Hits for suggested transient boundaries.
  4. Inspect the cuts on the waveform. Select Chops beside Play to hear the piano-roll arrangement.
  5. Try Randomise Slices or move individual notes while playback continues. Add effects if wanted, then export a WAV.

One Play/Stop button controls the main transport. Changing Sample/Chops during playback restarts playback in the selected mode.

02 · Map and edit the sample

Equal Slices divides the complete sample into 4, 8, 16 or 32 equal regions. Detect Hits proposes boundaries using changes in audio energy; sensitivity changes the detection threshold. Detection can return fewer slices than the selected count. Both actions replace the slice map and lay out a fresh sequence.

  • Click inside a waveform region to select it. Amber marks the edit selection; pastel green shows a sounding chopped hit. The moving line follows playback through that hit.
  • Drag an internal cut marker to move a shared boundary. Double-click to add a cut, or right-click near an internal cut to remove it.
  • Use Select Slice, start/end times and Set Boundaries for numerical editing. The first and last boundaries remain at the file edges.
  • Audition Slice previews the selected hit without gate or effects. Export Raw Slice saves that region separately.

The waveform stays pinned below the transport while you scroll. Up to 64 regions can be created by adding cuts manually. Check transient suggestions by ear and adjust them around the actual attack.

03 · Sequence and mash up the hits

Each piano-roll row triggers one slice. Rows are labelled from C2 upwards for identification; these labels do not provide external MIDI input or MIDI-file import.

  • Choose 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars beside the sequencer heading. Each bar contains 16 sixteenth-note positions. The full phrase fits the panel width.
  • Click an empty cell to add a note. Drag a note to change its time or slice; drag its right edge to change its duration.
  • Select a note and change Velocity or Note Length. Use Delete Selected, Delete/Backspace while the roll is focused, or right-click a note to remove it.
  • Lay Out Slices replaces the notes with slices distributed across the selected phrase. Increasing the bar count adds space; it does not duplicate the existing pattern. Reducing it removes notes outside the shorter phrase.
  • Groove Style / Apply Groove arranges mapped hits into structured patterns across the selected bar length. Families include four-on-the-floor, jungle, drum & bass, dub/skank/culture, ragga/riddim, trip-hop/phatness, big beat, techno, trance, house/garage, tribal and glitch. Variation A gives the foundation; B adds alternating-bar movement (or movement in a single bar); C adds an ending fill. Applying a built-in style sets swing, preserves cut markers and replaces the notes. Kick/snare/hat analysis is a suggestion; override its slice assignments in Hit Mapping. Undo Groove restores the arrangement from before the last application while the slice map is unchanged. All generated notes remain editable; no drums are synthesized or added.
  • Your Grooves saves your own edited notes, bar count and swing to this browser. Save under an existing name to update it. Recall from the style menu, or export/import a JSON bank for backup and transfer. Audio is not included; a matching slice map is required. Up to 128 custom grooves are supported.
  • Randomise Slices changes which hits are triggered while retaining note timing, duration and velocity. Randomise Rhythm builds new note positions and velocities using the available hits. Clear Notes empties the roll.

Editing and randomising keep the transport running. Changes apply to upcoming hits within the 150 ms scheduling window; hits already sounding are not rewound. An empty roll is silent in Chops mode. Tempo and gate-grid changes restart playback.

04 · Choose how slices play
One-shot
Plays the slice at its original speed. Piano-roll duration does not trim it.
Trim to note length
Plays at original speed, stopping at the note end or slice end, whichever comes first.
Fit to note
Changes playback speed so the complete slice fits the note. This also changes pitch; it is not pitch-preserving time stretch.
Choke previous slice
The next hit cuts off the preceding slice with a short fade. Turn this off to allow overlapping hits.

Whole-sample playback has its own Original speed / Fit to bar length controls in Gate / Motion. Fitting the whole sample also changes pitch. BPM controls sequencing and synchronised processing; it does not automatically detect the source tempo.

05 · Add gate, filter, distortion and echo

All four processing sections start off. Their switches bypass processing while retaining settings.

Gate / Motion
Enable the gate to impose an additional rhythm on the sample or chopped output. Tap gate steps to cycle full, ghost and closed levels. Edit Step / Step Level offers finer adjustment. Gate patterns have their own step grid and length, separate from the slice sequencer.
Gate behaviours
Pattern, incremental opening, incremental closing, swell, tempo pump, external sample sidechain, breakbeat ratchets and Euclidean rhythms. The external key controls the gate; it is not mixed into the audio. Choose original-speed or tempo-fit timing, then Open on hits, Follow sample loudness or Duck on hits. Normalize operator level helps quieter loops. Loading an operator enables the gate; its audio stays silent. Threshold sets the response. Drum-grid behaviours load editable kick, snare, hat, garage, jungle, dembow, Afro/clave and dub patterns; use the 1/16 step grid for their intended drumbeat timing.
Filter / Squelch
Choose a preset or select a low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, notch, peaking EQ, low-shelf, high-shelf or all-pass type. EQ Gain boosts or cuts peaking/shelf types. Cutoff and resonance remain manually adjustable. Open Filter Modulation / Automation for independent cutoff and resonance switches, phase, rate and depth. Shapes include smooth/linear rise-fall, rising/falling ramps, smoothed square, stepped rise and deterministic sample-and-hold. Cycle rates span 1/16 bar to 32 bars. Gate Filter Accents adds a gate-pattern envelope; it follows the gate grid, not individual slice-note velocities. Resonance movement uses the shared shape, cycle and phase around the manual resonance setting; shelves ignore resonance and all-pass is not amplitude-filtered. All automation starts off. Enabled movement is included in processed WAV export.
Distortion
Enable tape saturation, hard clip or foldback. Drive sets the intensity; Cutoff → Distortion Bite increases distortion as the cutoff opens.
Echo / Space
Choose a preset, then select centred mono feedback, alternating ping-pong, independent dual stereo (1 : 1.5 timing) or rhythmic multi-tap. Presets include dotted skank, jungle triplets, dub throw, half-time space, tribal taps and sixteenth scatter. Division, send, tone and stereo width shape the repeats; feedback is unused in multi-tap mode. Multi-tap sends five finite repeats at 0.5×, 1×, 1.5×, 2× and 3× the chosen division. Echoes can continue between gated hits. Quick Start scenes replace several gate/effect parameter settings; check section switches afterwards.
06 · Levels and troubleshooting

Open Input / Output · Levels & Audio Checks for input trim, output level and meters. Lift quiet samples can add up to 24 dB of source gain. Test Output plays a short tone; Audition Dry Sample previews the whole source with gate and effects bypassed. The main Play button returns to the selected Sample/Chops mode.

  • No chopped sound: choose Chops, check that the roll contains notes, and verify the selected slices contain audio.
  • Unexpected gaps: check the gate switch and pattern, choke setting and note positions.
  • Wrong speed or pitch: check Fit to note and whole-sample Fit to bar length settings.
  • Too much processing: bypass gate, filter, distortion and echo independently, then bring them back one at a time.
  • No sound anywhere: press Play after loading, check device/browser output, meters and output level, and try Test Output.
07 · Export and session handling

Export Processed WAV prints the currently selected Sample or Chops mode with the enabled processing, independently of dry audition.

  • Chop phrase length: renders the duration of the selected piano-roll phrase.
  • Sample length: renders the source duration; in whole-sample Fit mode it follows the fitted playback duration. In Chops mode it renders the arrangement for that duration.
  • Bar counts: render 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 or 32 bars. The selected arrangement repeats as required.
  • Include echo tail: adds a calculated decay allowance, up to 20 seconds. Normalize peak: scales the rendered peak before PCM conversion.
  • Export Raw Slice: saves only the selected source region at its original channel count and sample rate, without effects or normalization.

Use the download link if the browser does not save automatically. Use Save Session to download a JSON project containing all loaded track audio, notes, processing, tempo settings and the sixteen mute / solo scenes. Restore Session reopens that project; press Play to start. Reloading the HTML clears unsaved audio and edits, so save before closing. Your Grooves retains saved note patterns separately in browser storage, with JSON bank import/export. MIDI import/export is not included.

08 · Output mixer

All 16 tracks retain their independent EQ, dynamics, pan, level, mute, solo and track edit. Detailed parameters are visible as separate rows. Save/restore and mix export use the same channel processing. Console-style DSP, not a hardware-emulation claim.

One parameter per row, channel 01–16 across. Post-layer EQ → dynamics → shared bus. Low / high shelves: 120 Hz / 8 kHz; sweepable mid frequency and Q.

Live performance

Tap IN / OUT to queue a layer change. Hold CUT for an immediate drop; release to return. Loops keep their place. Scenes recall mute / solo only. Save Session keeps your scenes. Esc cancels the queue and releases cuts.

Timing and queue
Choose Immediate, Next beat, Next bar or Next 2 bars. Multiple queued track changes land together. Tap a queued track again to undo its change, or use Cancel to clear the queue. Clear Solos follows the selected timing.
Live waveforms
Each track pad displays its source waveform. The moving amber marker follows loop playback, including tempo changes. Muted or held-cut loops keep moving in time. Chopped and granular tracks show purple markers for active source reads. Stopped tracks display their waveform without a moving marker.
Sixteen scenes
Choose Store Scene, then a numbered slot to store the current mute / solo arrangement. Choosing an occupied slot while storing replaces that scene. Tap a saved slot to recall it using the selected timing. Levels, pan and processing stay as set. Saved slots are purple; matching scenes have an amber outline; queued recalls have a purple inset. Save Session includes all sixteen slots. Older four-scene sessions keep their original four slots.
Keyboard and cuts
Enable Keys for tracks 1–8; Shift + 1–8 controls tracks 9–16. Shortcuts operate while the mixer is visible and do not interrupt text or number entry. Hold a track’s Cut button with the pointer, Space or Enter; release to restore its current mute / solo state. Leaving the mixer, switching away from the browser or pressing Esc releases held cuts.
Live View
The mixer opens in Live View, with the performance pads, level faders and meters. Show Full Mixer reveals channel EQ, dynamics, pan and editing controls. Switching views retains every setting. Both labels use the same fixed button width.
09 · DECK workspace

DECK workspace · sixteen tracks

All sixteen tracks appear directly on DECK: tracks 1–8 in the left bank and 9–16 in the right bank. Each has a thin white source waveform, a thin off-white level slider, a level meter that changes from green through gold to red as the signal gets louder, IN / OUT, SOLO and hold-to-CUT controls. Select its number, name or waveform to target the central EQ, pan, filter, drive and echo functions. Effect switches show ON / OFF; their settings stay independent for every track. DECK dials follow the global Graphic Dials setting. DECK source waveforms use fine white strokes with an amber playhead. The shared OUTPUT meter reads the master bus after processing; a moving waveform alone does not establish audible output.

SABRE SAMPLES opens one shared compact loader on DECK or TRACKS, with an explicit target track. SABRE REPO beside each track’s local LOAD control opens the sample window beside that track and chooses its destination automatically. Folder groups start collapsed and build their contents only when opened; the cached index or fallback is available immediately while the full repository index refreshes. Search includes the TOTEM folders. DECK’s SABRE SAMPLES button also names its selected destination. Use Collapse for a small header, Expand to reopen, Close to dismiss, and Close after load to hide it after successful loading. Local file loading stays on TRACKS. The existing source loader keeps its playback rules: an empty track can join the mix; stop before replacing a source already playing.

The same menu and transport bar remain available on DECK, TRACKS, LAB, EFFECTS and MIXER, including Save Session and Load Session. Timing and all sixteen mute / solo scenes are shared with the mixer. Purple means queued, SOLO remains additive and respects OUT, and CUT acts only while held. Sessions retain the selected function target and support earlier two-deck sessions. Long samples retain their full duration; the views reuse existing audio players and waveform data.
